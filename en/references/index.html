OUR SERVICE AREAS & SECTOR SOLUTIONS

Our Corporate Solution Areas

We create maintenance-free biophilic botanical designs for architecture firms, hotels, corporate headquarters, healthcare clinics and exclusive residential projects.

Towers & Corporate Executive Floors

Sound-absorbing acoustic moss wall panels and corporate logo backdrops for executive offices, meeting rooms and reception areas.

Hotels & Hospitality

Monumental vertical gardens and handmade sculptural olive trees for high-ceilinged lobbies, courtyards and lounges.

Healthcare Centres & Private Clinics

100% hygienic natural moss arrangements that bring calm to patients and visitors and, because they need no soil or watering, harbour no insects or bacteria.

Restaurants, Cafés & Social Venues

Ceiling- and wall-integrated green botanical compositions tailored to the venue's concept, and decorative trees that form a focal point at its centre.

Architecture & Interior Design Partnerships

Seamless collaboration with interior designers and landscape studios, from the design and 3D visualisation stage to turnkey on-site installation.

Residences & Private Living Spaces

Custom-sized botanical art with brass and solid wood frames, plus green walls for entrance halls and living rooms in luxury villas and residences.
