Moss Wall, Vertical Garden and Interior Landscape Projects

A selection of installations we've brought to life with architects, interior designers and corporate clients. On every project, our own team handles the entire process: site survey, design, workshop production and installation.

A prestigious 42 m² acoustic wall with integrated hidden LED lighting, featuring ball moss and forest moss textures.

Vertical Garden Design
