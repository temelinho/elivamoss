Moss Walls for Offices and Stores: Why Are They So Popular?

Published: March 2026 • Reading Time: 6 Minutes • Author: Eliva Moss Architecture Group

Corporate architecture has been undergoing a radical transformation in recent years. Cold grey office towers, sterile white walls and overly technological atmospheres are giving way to biophilic architecture — organic design elements that reconnect people with nature. And the strongest representative of this movement is, without doubt, preserved moss walls.

Why do thousands of businesses — from Google, Apple, luxury financial institutions and boutique law firms to healthcare clinics — choose moss walls for their lobbies and open-plan offices? Here are the 4 key architectural and corporate reasons.

1. Acoustic Insulation: A Natural Solution to Echo in Open-Plan Offices

In open-plan layouts, glass partitions, epoxy or parquet floors and hard concrete ceilings cause sound to reverberate constantly. Phone calls, keyboard clatter and meeting chatter make it impossible for employees to focus.

At a microscopic level, moss has a spongy, open-pored fibre structure. A preserved moss panel on the wall prevents sound waves from bouncing back, absorbing mid- and high-frequency sound by up to 30%. Instead of using unsightly acoustic foam, you turn your office wall into both an art gallery and a natural sound insulator.

2. Employee Productivity and Stress Reduction

Research on biophilic workplaces by the Universities of Harvard and Exeter has revealed these striking results in offices enriched with natural greenery:

  • Employees' feelings of stress and burnout drop by 37%.
  • Concentration and problem-solving ability increase by 15%.
  • A notable decrease is observed in sick leave and headache complaints.

Focusing on natural shades of green rests the eyes from the strain caused by the blue light of computer screens and instantly gives the mind the calm of a walk in the forest.

3. Corporate Prestige and Brand Identity (First Impressions)

Within the first 10 seconds of stepping into your lobby, clients or investors form a subconscious judgement about your brand. Seeing the company's metal logo on a floor-to-ceiling, richly textured, backlit moss wall with a brushed brass frame proclaims a modern, sustainable, prestigious and environmentally conscious vision — without a single word.

4. Zero Operating Costs: No Plumbing, No Gardener

Many companies that install living vertical gardens run into these problems within 6 months:

  • Drip irrigation systems clogging or leaking water onto office carpets,
  • Plants yellowing and drying out due to insufficient daylight,
  • High monthly maintenance costs and expenses for replacing dead plants.

Eliva Moss preserved moss walls, by contrast, are delivered with a one-time installation. They require no water pipes, electric pumps or drainage, and keep their first-day beauty for 7 to 10 years without a penny of extra cost.
