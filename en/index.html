FREQUENTLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our customers ask most about our preserved moss designs.

Preserved mosses are real, natural plants. Through a special ecological preservation process, the plant's sap is replaced with natural glycerine and minerals. This way it keeps its first-day freshness and soft texture for 7 to 10 years without photosynthesising, needing water or drying out.
Absolutely not. Preserved moss must never be watered or sprayed with water. It needs no soil, fertiliser or daylight. Standard indoor relative humidity (40–70%) is perfectly sufficient to keep its texture supple and soft.
Natural preserved moss has an antistatic structure, so it doesn't generate static electricity or attract dust. Airing it from a distance once or twice a year with a light dusting brush or a low-power cool hairdryer is all the cleaning it needs.
