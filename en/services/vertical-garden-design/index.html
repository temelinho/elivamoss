VERTICAL GARDEN

Vertical Garden Design and Installation

We design green surfaces that turn a blank wall into the most talked-about spot in the room. Together, we choose the right plant system for the space's light, how heavily it's used and the level of upkeep you expect.

VERTICAL GARDEN DESIGN

Green Surfaces Designed Around Your Space

No two vertical gardens are alike. A corridor with no daylight and a lobby with a glass façade call for different solutions. That's why we always start by getting to know the space: light, humidity, ventilation and the wall's load-bearing capacity form the foundation of the project.

We recommend whatever suits your needs best: maintenance-free blends of preserved plants and moss, artificial plant systems that are hard to tell from the real thing, or hybrid designs that combine both. With logo, lighting and frame details, we make your vertical garden part of your brand.

HOW WE WORK

Four Steps from Site Survey to Installation

01

Survey and Project Planning

We listen to your needs by phone or through an on-site survey, then prepare drawings and visual options based on measurements, photos and expectations.

02

Design Workshop

The approved design is produced one-to-one in our workshop. Moss, plants and natural branches are placed by hand, and the finished work is presented for your approval.

03

Packaging and Shipping

Products are shipped with special packaging that protects their texture, along with installation templates.

04

Installation and Handover

Because panels are prepared in the workshop, only installation is done on site, so your space is ready to use in no time.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

Most projects require no additional construction work. Panels are prepared in the workshop and fixed to the wall in modules. During the site survey, we examine the wall structure and choose the most suitable installation method.
Preserved systems are made from real plants, give the most natural look and are intended for indoor use. Artificial systems, thanks to their UV resistance, can also be used outdoors and in sunny areas.
The price varies with the square metres, the type of plants used, the density of the design and the installation conditions. The dimensions and a few photos are enough for us to prepare a preliminary quote.
