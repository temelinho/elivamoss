Preserved Moss Wall Installations

Using preserved moss that needs no watering, pruning or daylight, we transform your walls into surfaces that keep their form for years, soften sound and bring a natural serenity to your space.

MOSS WALLS

Zero Maintenance, Long Life, Real Texture

Preserved moss is real moss gathered from nature and preserved through a special process. The plant's sap is replaced with a natural glycerine-based solution, so the moss stays as it was on day one for years without losing its softness or colour.

By combining different textures such as flat moss, ball moss and lichen, we create compositions unique to each space. The fibrous structure of the moss surface absorbs sound and reduces echo, making a moss wall both an aesthetic and acoustic solution, especially for open-plan offices, meeting rooms and restaurants.

HOW WE WORK

Four Steps from Site Survey to Installation

01

Survey and Project Planning

We listen to your needs by phone or through an on-site survey, then prepare drawings and visual options based on measurements, photos and expectations.

02

Design Workshop

The approved design is produced one-to-one in our workshop. Moss, plants and natural branches are placed by hand, and the finished work is presented for your approval.

03

Packaging and Shipping

Products are shipped with special packaging that protects their texture, along with installation templates.

04

Installation and Handover

Because panels are prepared in the workshop, only installation is done on site, so your space is ready to use in no time.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

Under suitable indoor conditions (away from direct sun and water contact, 40–70% humidity), preserved moss walls keep their form and colour for 7–10 years.
Thanks to its antistatic structure, preserved moss doesn't attract dust and produces no pollen. An occasional light brush or a blast of cool air is all the cleaning it needs.
Preserved moss should not be exposed to direct water or sun. For such areas, we recommend artificial vertical garden systems.
