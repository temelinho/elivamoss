Handmade Decorative Trees

We produce decorative trees to project-specific sizes for high-ceilinged lobbies, atriums and restaurants. Every detail, from trunk to leaf, is crafted by hand for a realistic look.

HANDMADE DECORATIVE TREES

A Tree at the Heart of the Space

A large tree can change the entire atmosphere of a space. But growing a living tree indoors is often impossible. Our handmade decorative trees fill that gap: they stay as they were on day one for years, with no need for light, watering or maintenance.

We produce olive, plane tree, cherry blossom and similar forms, custom-sized to the ceiling height and concept of the space. The trunk structure, branch angle and foliage density are decided together with the project.

HOW WE WORK

Four Steps from Site Survey to Installation

01

Survey and Project Planning

We listen to your needs by phone or through an on-site survey, then prepare drawings and visual options based on measurements, photos and expectations.

02

Design Workshop

The approved design is produced one-to-one in our workshop. Moss, plants and natural branches are placed by hand, and the finished work is presented for your approval.

03

Packaging and Shipping

Products are shipped with special packaging that protects their texture, along with installation templates.

04

Installation and Handover

Because panels are prepared in the workshop, only installation is done on site, so your space is ready to use in no time.
