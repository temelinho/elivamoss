Triangular Botanical Moss Art 06

This product is available in different sizes and frame options. Message us for options and price differences.

Triangular Botanical Moss Art 06 is a single-piece artwork with a triangular shape and a dark walnut wood frame that adds natural depth to your walls. It combines preserved flat moss, ball (pillow) moss, lichen and natural branches; every piece is placed by hand in our workshop.

Real preserved moss needs no watering, soil or daylight, and keeps its natural softness and colour indoors for many years. Its fibrous structure softens sound, giving the room a calm atmosphere.
